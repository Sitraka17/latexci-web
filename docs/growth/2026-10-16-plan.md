# Growth plan, 16 October 2026

Decisions taken on 16/10 (asked by Sitraka: "answer each question yourself, for growth, and do it").

## 1. Word to LaTeX without sign-in: first file free (done, 4278806)
- Why: /tools/word-to-latex is a top search page. Asking for an account before the tool has proved
  anything loses most visitors, and the conversion runs in the browser, so it costs nothing.
- How: one anonymous conversion per browser (signed cookie `lx_wf`), sign-in from the second.
  The sign-in prompt appears right under the result of the free conversion.
- Measure: admin table, Word column, and the share of new accounts after 2 to 4 weeks.

## 2. Product-news emails: yes, opt-in (done, 4278806)
- Unticked checkbox under every "Sign in with Google" button; stored on the user record
  (`newsletter.optIn`, date). Toggle in /dashboard, column in the admin table and CSV.
- Nothing is sent yet. First email only when there is real news (new tool), at most monthly.

## 3. Favourite symbols: yes, cheap retention (done)
- localStorage only, no account. Star on each symbol page, "Favourites" block at the top of the hub.

## 6. ISR reads (Vercel)
- The "60 %" is latexci's share (525k) of the team's 886k ISR read units over 30 days, against a
  Hobby allowance of 1M for all projects. Team CDN requests were 1.54M against a 1M allowance.
- Read units are counted per 8 KB served from the ISR cache, so they follow page weight times
  traffic. Fully static export is not possible (sign-in and PDF API routes). Done: the 404 body
  left the RSC payload of every page (about -16 KB, -14 to -17 % per page); links prefetch on
  intent. Lower reads change nothing for visitors: same pages, same speed.
- If traffic doubles, the honest fix is Vercel Pro (10M ISR reads, 10M CDN requests), not more
  trimming.
